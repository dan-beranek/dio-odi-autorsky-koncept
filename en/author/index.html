Authorial position

A concept led by one author—not an anonymous “team.”

Daniel Beránek leads the conceptual, strategic, research and communication layers of DIO/ODI.

He articulated the concept from professional practice spanning meaning, sales, journalism, copywriting, SEO, web technologies and digital identities. His academic background in psychology and communication studies made it possible to connect practical representation problems with questions of interpretation, trust and relationships.

He does not present technical, institutional or capital capabilities that the project does not yet possess. He seeks them according to the specific delivery track and states clearly where additional capacity is needed.

Origins of the concept

Practice → articulation → manifesto → research and application architecture.

DIO did not begin as a name in search of a problem. It emerged from recognizing a recurring object of work: an entity whose meaning must persist across people and different machine systems.

Practice

More than thirteen years of work with meaning, SEO, content, the web, brands, reputation and digital communication.

Articulation

Recognition that seemingly separate tasks repeatedly address the same deeper problem: how an entity is represented, interpreted and reconstructed.

Manifest

Naming DIO and ODI, creating a shared terminology and publishing a canonical multilingual edition.

Formalization

Conceptual development, the K6 model, thematic concepts and the distinction between applied DIO and theoretical ODI.

Delivery architecture

Three value tracks, an intervention design and paths to pilots, co-development, research or licensing.
