DIO/ODI · an author-led concept by Daniel Beránek

The new object of optimization is not a page. It is the entity.

DIO shapes how companies, people, products and projects are recognized, connected and interpreted across human audiences, search engines, AI and data systems. ODI formalizes the principles behind this reconstruction as a distinct theoretical and research layer.

Shared mechanism

Each system reconstructs the same entity from a different set of traces.

Digital identity is neither a single profile nor a database row. It is a distributed object, repeatedly reconstructed from the representations, relationships and evidence available to each observer.

K6 semantic connectome

Ways to engage

A partner brings a specific problem, environment or capability.

DIO/ODI offers defined development paths. The scope of collaboration and any resulting rights are negotiated according to the track, the objective and each party’s actual contribution.

Capital, infrastructure or market access for a clearly defined thesis.

The value of early participation

Not free exclusivity. Real influence on the first operational form.

A first partner can co-create a domain application and gain early experience with the methodology before it is standardized.

  • Direct access to the author and emerging know-how
  • An opportunity to shape the application’s first operational form
  • Reference or founding status when supported by a substantive contribution
  • An opportunity to negotiate deeper collaboration and broader rights

A pilot does not by itself grant a license, ownership or exclusivity. The foundational IP and authorship remain with Daniel Beránek unless a specific agreement states otherwise.
